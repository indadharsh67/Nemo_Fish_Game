🐠 Nemo's Ocean Escape
A fast-paced, addictive arcade game inspired by Finding Nemo! Dive into the deep blue, dodge hungry predators, and rack up the highest score you can.

🎮 About the Game
You play as a little fish navigating the dangerous open ocean. Your goal is simple: survive and feast!
Swim around to collect as many tasty worms as possible to boost your score, all while outsmarting and escaping two relentless sharks patrolling the waters. One wrong move and it's lunch time!

🕹️ Controls
Navigate the ocean using the on-screen joystick:
Front (Up): Swim forward / up
Back (Down): Swim backward / down
Left: Swim left
Right: Swim right

🏆 How to Win (and Survive!)
Collect Worms: Grab every worm you see to increase your score.
Watch Your Tail: Keep an eye on both sharks at all times—they are always on the hunt.
Beat Your High Score: Stay alive as long as possible to become the ultimate ocean survivor!
